Bangla type

Noto Sans Bengali at the body and largest heading sizes of the Ink scale. Bangla is never below 13 px or a 1.4 line height. Look for cut tops on ঊ, ঐ and রেফ, and cut bottoms on ু, ূ and ৃ.
