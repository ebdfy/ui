ebdfy design system

Components

Web components for the ebdfy Admin and the apps inside it. Add one script, then write the elements in any framework. 

<script type="module" src="https://cdn.ebdfy.com/ui/v1.js"></script>

Foundations

The Ink colours in light and dark, the type scale in English and Bangla, spacing, radii, depth and icons.

Button

eb-button: an action, primary, secondary, ghost or danger, at 28, 36 or 44 px, and 44 px on a phone.

Card

eb-card: a surface with a fine ring that groups related content.

Page

eb-page: the background and title of an app's page.

Heading

eb-heading: a page, section or card title at three levels.

Text

eb-text: body text, and quieter meta text.

Badge

eb-badge: a status as a dot and a word.

Link

eb-link: an underlined link inside a sentence.

Text field

eb-text-field: a line of text, with its label and error.

Text area

eb-text-area: a few lines of text, such as a note.

Select

eb-select: one pick from a short list.

Search field

eb-search-field: a search box the save bar never counts.

Date field

eb-date-field: a day, as YYYY-MM-DD.

Phone field

eb-phone-field: a Bangladesh mobile number, kept in Latin digits.

Taka field

eb-taka-field: an amount in taka, grouped in lakhs.

District picker

eb-district-picker: one of the 64 districts and its area.

Checkbox

eb-checkbox: a yes or no, with a partly checked state.

Choice list

eb-choice-list: one pick, drawn as radio buttons.

Switch

eb-switch: a setting turned on or off.

Banner

eb-banner: a note or a risk, in the page.

Pagination

eb-pagination: previous and next buttons for pages by cursor.

Empty state

eb-empty-state: why a list is empty, and what to do next.

Skeleton

eb-skeleton: grey lines that hold the place of text while it loads.

Thumbnail

eb-thumbnail: a product picture in a small square, with an icon when it is missing.

Avatar

eb-avatar: a person as a circle, with their initials when there is no picture.

Courier status

eb-courier-status: what a courier says happened to a parcel.

Bulk bar

eb-bulk-bar: the actions for the orders a person selected.

Tooltip

eb-tooltip: a short text above its trigger, on hover or focus.

Menu

eb-menu and eb-menu-item: a list of actions that opens from a button, with shortcut hints.

Popover

eb-popover: a panel of any content that opens below a button.

Kbd

eb-kbd: a keyboard shortcut, Ctrl+D here and ⌘D on a Mac.

Bottom bar

eb-bottom-bar: buttons that stay at the bottom of the scrolling page.

Toast

A short message the Admin chrome shows at the foot of the window, with an Undo action or as an error.

Modal

An app's own page in a small, medium or large window over the Admin, a bottom sheet on a phone.

Admin chrome

eb-admin-chrome: the title bar and navigation that frame an app.

Table

eb-table and eb-row: orders as a table on a computer and cards on a phone, with an open row and selection.

List

eb-list: a column of cards on any screen.

Stack

eb-stack: children in a column with the standard gap, or side by side in a row.

Grid

eb-grid: equal columns, and one column on a phone.

Divider

eb-divider: a 1 px line between two parts of a page.

Button group

eb-button-group: related buttons in one labelled row that wraps.

Progress bar

eb-progress-bar: how far some work has got, or a moving bar when that is not known.

Spinner

eb-spinner: a small turning ring while something loads.
