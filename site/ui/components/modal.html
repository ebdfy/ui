ebdfy design system

Modal

An app opens a modal with ebdfy.modal, and the Admin chrome draws it over the app. The page inside is one of the app's own pages. In the frame below, press Book courier in the title bar.

On a desktop the sizes are small at 380 px, medium at 620 px and large at 980 px wide. On a phone the modal is a bottom sheet with a grab handle. Escape, the close button or a tap on the backdrop closes it, and focus goes back to what opened it. Only a path on the app's own origin opens.

The chrome's own words (such as Close) follow the page's lang, not the language row below. The row changes only this page's own strings.
