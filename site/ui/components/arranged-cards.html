Arranged cards

e-arranged-cards holds a page's cards in the order the staff member chose, such as Home's and Reports'. It lays them on four columns from 600 px of its own width: a card takes the columns its span asks for, all four when it asks none, and below 600 px a one-column card takes half the row. Each card carries its id in card.

A button whose aria-controls names the part, such as Customize, turns on edit mode, which v2-host.js builds in the Admin: Reset to default and Done above the cards, a strip above each card with a drag handle and Remove, or Move up and Move down when the part is narrower than 600 px, and an Add card tile at the end. Add card opens the e-modal that library names, whose buttons carry each card's id in card and its heading; it offers only the cards the page does not show. A picked card the page does not hold shows as a placeholder until the page draws it. Done writes the card ids, in order, to the field name names and submits the form around the part, so the page saves them and draws its cards in that order.
