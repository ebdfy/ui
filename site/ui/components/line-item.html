Line item

e-line-item draws one order line: an optional e-thumbnail, the product as slot="title", its variant as slot="meta", the quantity and price as slot="price" and the line's amount as slot="amount". Where the box holding the lines is 480 px or wider it is one row, and amounts line up down consecutive lines. Narrower, as on a phone or beside a record's side column, the amount ends the title's line and the variant and price go under it. A long title wraps.
