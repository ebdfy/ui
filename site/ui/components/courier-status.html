Courier status

eb-courier-status shows what a courier says happened to a parcel. Its status is the courier's code, such as IN_TRANSIT; the element writes the words in the page's language and picks the badge tone itself. A code it does not know reads as unknown.

English বাংলা

Five orders, each with its courier status. 

<eb-courier-status status="PENDING"></eb-courier-status>
<eb-courier-status status="IN_TRANSIT"></eb-courier-status>
<eb-courier-status status="OUT_FOR_DELIVERY"></eb-courier-status>
<eb-courier-status status="DELIVERED"></eb-courier-status>
<eb-courier-status status="RETURNED"></eb-courier-status>
