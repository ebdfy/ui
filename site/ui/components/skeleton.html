Skeleton

e-skeleton holds the place of text while it loads. Set lines from 2 to 8 for that many lines of body text, the last one shorter; without it the skeleton is one line. A screen reader finds nothing in it, so say the loading state elsewhere. Set shape="table" for a toolbar, a head row and 8 rows of 44 px, or shape="details" for two cards at their real heights; lines is ignored with a shape.
