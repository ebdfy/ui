Button

eb-button runs an action. Use variant="primary" for the one main action of a card, and the default secondary for the rest. A button is as wide as its label; full-width makes it fill its row, as two equal actions in a bar do. A toggle, such as the current filter, sets pressed to true or false, so a screen reader hears whether it is on, and its fill goes a step deeper while it is on. disabled locks a button: it ignores clicks and Tab skips it. loading shows a spinner in place of the label while its action runs, at the same width, and ignores clicks until it is done. An eb-icon in slot="icon" sits before the label; icon-only makes a square button that shows only the icon, named by its label.
