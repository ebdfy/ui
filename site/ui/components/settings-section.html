Saving with the Admin's bar

Put the sections of a settings page in one <form data-save-bar>. While a named field holds an edit, the Admin shows one save bar in its top band, with Discard and Save; on a phone it takes the whole band. Save submits the form, as do Ctrl+S or ⌘S and Enter in a one-line field; Enter in a text area is a new line, and Esc never discards. Leaving the page with an edit asks first. In an app, v2.js tells the Admin through the bridge's saveBar message, and ebdfy.showErrors(form, userErrors) turns the bar to "Not saved: 1 field needs a fix".

A form that makes a record names the bar's words: data-save-message is its message, such as Unsaved order, and marks the form as new, so Discard asks first with data-save-discard; data-save-label is Save's word, such as Create order. A hidden <button type="submit" data-save-bar-button> in the form is a second button in the bar from 1040 px, such as Save as draft. A form for a record that is not saved yet, such as a new report the page filled in, carries data-save-new: the bar shows from the first frame and Save sends the form as it is. Discard puts the form's values back and fires e-save-discarded on it. On the Admin's own pages a save that meets an expired session fires e-session-expired on the form, and the bar sends it again when the page fires e-session-restored.
