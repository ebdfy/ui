Banner

eb-banner puts a message in the page: tone="info", the default, for a note, and tone="danger" for a risk to act on, such as a customer whose orders keep coming back. Its icon carries a name for screen readers; the banner does not interrupt them.

English বাংলা
Pathao picks up orders booked before 4 pm the same day. 3 of this number's 5 orders came back. Take the delivery charge in advance by bKash.
