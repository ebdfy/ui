Banner

eb-banner puts a message in the page: tone="info", the default, for a note, and tone="danger" for a risk to act on, such as a customer whose orders keep coming back. Its icon carries a name for screen readers; the banner does not interrupt them. heading adds a title above the words, an element with slot="action" sits after them, and dismissible adds a close button that fires eb-dismiss, which a listener can cancel to keep the banner.
