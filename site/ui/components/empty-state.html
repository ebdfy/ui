ebdfy design system

Empty state

eb-empty-state tells a person why a list has nothing in it and what to do next. Its heading is the title, a paragraph inside it explains, and an element with slot="action" is the next step.

English বাংলা

Orders you start but do not confirm are kept here.

</eb-empty-state>
