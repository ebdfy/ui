Menu

eb-menu opens a list of actions from the eb-button in its trigger slot. Each action is an eb-menu-item. Set tone="critical" on a risky one, and shortcut to show its keys at the end of the row. The arrow keys move between items, Enter picks one and Escape closes the menu. The page shows Ctrl here and ⌘ on a Mac or iPhone.
