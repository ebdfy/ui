Bulk bar

eb-bulk-bar offers what a person can do to the orders they selected. Set count to the number selected; at zero the bar hides. Put small ghost buttons inside it as the actions, each with an eb-icon in its icon slot. It sends eb-clear when the close button is pressed.

Place it last in the list's column, after the list and its pagination. It floats 16 px above the bottom of the screen over the rows, and showing it moves nothing in the list; at the end of the scroll it sits below the pagination. On a phone it keeps the first action and puts the others in a ⋯ menu. The frame below keeps the example from floating over this page.
