Bulk bar

eb-bulk-bar offers what a person can do to the orders they selected. Set count to the number selected; at zero the bar hides. Put small ghost buttons inside it as the actions. It sends eb-clear when the close button is pressed.

English বাংলা
Confirm Book courier Print labels 
<eb-bulk-bar count="2">
  <eb-button variant="ghost" size="sm">Confirm</eb-button>
  <eb-button variant="ghost" size="sm">Book courier</eb-button>
  <eb-button variant="ghost" size="sm">Print labels</eb-button>
</eb-bulk-bar>
