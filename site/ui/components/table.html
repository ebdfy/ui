eb-table lays out orders as a table on a screen 600 px wide or more and as one list of rows split by dividers below it. Write an eb-column for each heading, with card naming its place on a phone's card (title, end, meta or status), and an eb-row whose children are its cells. A column with grow="2" takes twice the width of one without, which takes 1 share; a phone's cards ignore it. A column with align="end", such as a total, ends its heading and cells on the right, and every cell draws even-width digits, so amounts line up. A cell stays on one line and ends in an ellipsis when its column is too narrow, with its whole text as its title. As the table narrows, a column with priority="3" steps aside below 900 px and one with priority="2" below 720 px, so keep the order, customer, status and total at the default priority. Rows are 44 px, or 40 px with density="compact". A row with href opens that address: its first cell is a link to it, a click elsewhere on the row follows it, and the row tints under the pointer. A row with open carries the accent bar of the order on screen beside the list; a selectable table leads with checkboxes, tints a selected row and fires eb-selection. A row's child with card="note" shows under its card on a phone. Rows carry no avatars.
