Chart

e-chart is a column chart of one value a bar, such as net sales a day. The page writes every part, so v2.css draws it in the first frame and the script never moves it: the axis's e-chart-steps, top to bottom, each label on its gridline, then an e-chart-bar a value, its height the share of the axis written as style="--e-share: 0.62". A bar's text is its value, which a screen reader reads after its label; tick shows the label under the axis, and today draws the bar in lime. Put it in an e-card.
