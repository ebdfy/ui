Menu tree

e-menu-tree arranges a menu's items, up to 3 levels deep. Each e-menu-tree-item is one row: a drag handle, its label, and Edit and Delete. Children sit indented under their item, and each group ends in Add menu item to that item. It is the Admin's, so v2-host.js builds it, not v2.js.

Drag a row by its handle to put it before or after another row, or onto the middle of one to put it under it; its children go with it. On the handle, Up and Down move it among its siblings, Right puts it under the item above, and Left lifts it out of its parent. A move that would make a fourth level is refused, and the row says so; a drop onto the item's own children does nothing.

Inside a form, the tree keeps a hidden field named name holding its items as the API takes them, [{"title":"Home","url":"/","items":[]}, …], so the save bar counts every change and Discard puts the items back. Add and Edit fire e-menu-tree-add and e-menu-tree-edit: the page opens its own modal, with the store's link search, and calls detail.done with the item.
